Can I edit a scanned PDF or add text to a scanned document?

You can use the available text box feature to add new text over a scanned PDF page. However, scanned documents often contain images of pages rather than selectable text.

This means that adding text to a scanned page does not automatically recognize or modify the words already present in the image. If you need to edit existing scanned text, a separate Optical Character Recognition (OCR) tool may be necessary.

Does the Whiteout tool permanently redact confidential PDF information?

No. The Whiteout tool places an opaque coloured rectangle over visible page content. It is useful for visual covering, annotations, and preparing a document layout, but it does not guarantee that the original underlying text or image has been removed from the PDF.

Do not rely on this visual Whiteout feature to protect passwords, identification numbers, financial details, or other sensitive information. Use a dedicated secure redaction workflow that removes the underlying content and verify the sanitized file before sharing it.

Is adding a signature image the same as digitally signing a PDF?

No. These are two different ways of signing a document. A signature image adds a visual representation of your handwritten signature to the PDF page.

A certificate-based digital signature uses cryptographic technology to help verify the identity associated with the signature and detect changes to the signed document.

For routine documents, a signature image may be suitable when the recipient accepts it. For official agreements, contracts, or business-critical paperwork, confirm which signing method is required before submitting the document.

Is it safe to upload confidential PDF documents online?

Document privacy is important, especially when working with personal identification, financial records, confidential business information, or legal paperwork.

Before uploading sensitive documents to any online PDF editor, review its privacy policy and understand how uploaded files are processed, stored, and deleted. Avoid submitting highly confidential information unless you are comfortable with the tool's security and privacy practices.

For particularly sensitive documents, consider using a trusted offline PDF application instead.

Working with PDF files does not have to be complicated. A little preparation can save time, prevent mistakes, and help you maintain better-organized documents. Whether you are editing an assignment, preparing office paperwork, splitting a lengthy report, or joining several documents, these simple habits can make a difference.
